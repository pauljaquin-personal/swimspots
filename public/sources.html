Sources

Swimspots brings several kinds of public information together in one place. We show sources so you can check the original information when it matters.

Water quality

Swimming-water information is linked to LAWA and, where available, the relevant regional or local council service such as ORC, Environment Southland or Auckland Safeswim.

Weather and sea conditions

Forecast weather and marine values are modelled information rather than observations at the exact swimming point. Forecasts are useful context, but local conditions can differ.

Maps and places

The standard map uses OpenStreetMap data. Satellite imagery is provided through Esri and its imagery contributors. Public-toilet searches use OpenStreetMap data.

Local and community information

Access, parking, facilities, hazards, photographs and descriptions may come from official public sources, editorial research or reviewed community contributions. Individual listings identify sources where practical.

Limitations

  • Map coordinates and entry points may be approximate.
  • Access, facilities, hazards and closures can change.
  • Forecast values are models, not measurements at the swimming location.
  • Water-quality results may be based on samples taken earlier and at a particular monitoring point.
  • Community information may be incomplete even when it has been reviewed for publication.

For decisions about current water quality, warnings, closures or emergencies, use the relevant official source and information at the location.
