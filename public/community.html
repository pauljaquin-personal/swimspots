Community

Swimspots gets better when swimmers share useful local knowledge and tell us when something has changed.

Contribute a spot

Know somewhere that should be on the map? Return to the map and tap the + button to suggest a location. Suggestions are reviewed before publication.

Add local knowledge

You can contribute access details, facilities, hazards and photographs to existing locations. Please share information that is appropriate to make public and avoid private access details or personal information.

Corrections and contact

If a listing is wrong, out of date or missing something important, use the Report action on that spot. A dedicated contact form is planned as we prepare for wider beta testing.
