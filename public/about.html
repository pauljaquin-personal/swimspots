About

Swimspots is a simple way to discover open-water swimming places around Aotearoa New Zealand.

What you’ll find here

Each spot brings together the practical information you are most likely to want before a swim: where it is, how to get in, known hazards, facilities, weather, water quality and useful local sources.

How it works

Swimspots combines public and official information with local knowledge. We try to show where information came from and keep modelled conditions separate from observations and water-quality samples.

Still growing

The map is an early and growing collection. Some locations have richer information than others, and access or conditions can change. If you know a spot well, your corrections and local knowledge can make the listing more useful.
